================================================================================
KINGDOM LEGACY — GAME DESIGN OUTLINE
================================================================================
Engine: Godot 4.7 (GDScript)        Art: 32x32 pixel art (PixelLab)
View: Top-down 2D                   Status: Early prototype
Last updated: 2026-09-22


1. PITCH
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Grow a medieval hamlet into a thriving kingdom, then defend it. A deep city-
building sim in the spirit of SimCity / Cities: Skylines / Banished, where every
villager is a visible person hauling goods along the roads you lay, and where
goblins, orcs and trolls raid in escalating waves that test the town you built.


2. PILLARS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  - A LIVING TOWN: every citizen is an agent you can watch. Goods physically
    move. Bottlenecks are visible, not just numbers.
  - ROADS AND LAYOUT MATTER: distance, road networks and placement drive
    efficiency. A well-planned town outperforms a sprawling one.
  - THE ECONOMY FEEDS THE ARMY: troops, walls and weapons come from supply
    chains. A strong economy is your best defense.
  - EARNED ESCALATION: raids grow with your kingdom. Expansion is always a
    risk/reward decision.


3. CORE LOOP
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  Build housing -> villagers arrive -> assign them to jobs (automatic)
    -> gather raw resources -> process into goods -> satisfy needs
    -> population grows & settlement tier rises -> unlock buildings/research
    -> raids grow stronger -> build defenses & train troops -> survive -> repeat


4. WIN / LOSE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  Goal-based. Default scenario goals (tunable per map):
    - WIN:  Reach the Kingdom tier AND survive the final great raid
            (e.g. a troll warlord / dragon siege).
    - LOSE: The Keep is destroyed, OR population falls to zero.
  Future: scenario-specific goals (survive N raids, reach X population,
  build a Cathedral, etc.).


5. THE MAP
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  - Procedurally generated each game (128x128 tiles, seed shown for sharing).
  - Terrain: Water, Sand, Grass, Forest, Stone. Planned: Hills, Iron/Gold ore,
    Fertile soil, Rivers, Swamp.
  - Resource tiles deplete (forests -> grass). Planned: forest regrowth,
    forester building to replant.
  - Start area is guaranteed clear with forest and stone nearby.
  - Raid spawn points sit at the map edges (planned: monster lairs/caves
    placed in the wilds that can be assaulted to reduce raid pressure).


6. CITIZENS (AGENT SIMULATION)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  Each villager has: a home, an optional job, a carried item, and a state.
  - Spawn: move into houses with free space and road access.
  - Jobs: auto-assigned to the nearest open workplace.
  - Work: walk to resource / workplace, produce, carry goods to storage.
  - Movement: A* pathfinding. Roads are ~1.6x faster; forests slower.

  Planned needs (drive happiness, growth and productivity):
    Tier 1  Food, Shelter
    Tier 2  Water (well), Firewood, Religion (chapel)
    Tier 3  Varied food, Market access, Tavern
    Tier 4  Clothing, Healthcare, Safety (guard coverage)
  Unmet needs -> unhappiness -> slower work, emigration, eventually unrest.
  Planned: day/night cycle (villagers go home at night), aging, births.


7. ECONOMY & SUPPLY CHAINS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  Raw resources:  Wood, Stone, Wheat, Fish, Game, Iron ore, Clay, Wool, Gold ore
  Storage:        Keep (starts), Stockpile (raw), Granary (food), Warehouse

  Chains (-> = building transforms input to output):
    Wood         Woodcutter                          [BUILT]
    Stone        Quarry                              [BUILT]
    Bread        Farm -> Wheat -> Mill -> Flour -> Bakery -> Bread   [BUILT]
    Fish         Fisher's Hut (coast) -> Fish                       [BUILT]
    Meat         Hunter's Lodge -> Game -> Butcher -> Meat
    Planks       Wood -> Sawmill -> Planks
    Tools        Iron Mine -> Ore -> Smelter -> Iron -> Blacksmith -> Tools
    Weapons      Iron -> Weaponsmith -> Swords / Spears
    Bows         Wood -> Fletcher -> Bows / Arrows
    Armor        Iron -> Armorer -> Armor
    Cloth        Sheep Farm -> Wool -> Weaver -> Cloth
    Bricks       Clay Pit -> Clay -> Kiln -> Bricks
    Gold         Market / Taxes on houses; Gold Mine -> Mint

  Haulers: later, a dedicated hauler job moves goods between buildings and
  storage so producers keep working.
  Gold: collected as tax from happy households; spent on troops' upkeep,
  research and advanced buildings.


8. SETTLEMENT TIERS & RESEARCH
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  Tier        Requirement (draft)              Unlocks (draft)
  ----------  -------------------------------  --------------------------------
  Hamlet      Start                            House, Woodcutter, Quarry,
                                               Stockpile, Farm, Well
  Village     20 pop, food + water met         Mill, Bakery, Chapel, Market,
                                               Palisade, Watchtower, Militia
  Town        60 pop, + religion + market      Sawmill, Smithy, Stone walls,
                                               Barracks, Archers, Tavern
  City        150 pop, + tavern + clothing     Castle upgrade, Knights, Guilds,
                                               Siege defenses, Hospital
  Kingdom     300 pop, + all needs, castle     Cathedral, Elite troops,
                                               final raid triggers

  Research: done at a Library/Scholar's Hall with gold + time. Examples:
    Crop Rotation (+farm yield), Masonry (stone walls), Wheelbarrows
    (+carry amount), Longbows (archer range), Tempered Steel (+troop damage).


9. DEFENSE & COMBAT (HYBRID)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  Troops auto-defend by default; player may take direct control of squads.

  Defensive structures:
    Palisade (wood wall), Stone Wall, Gate (villagers pass, enemies don't),
    Watchtower (archer slot), Guard Tower, Moat, Spike trap, Castle upgrade.

  Troops (trained at Barracks; cost gold + equipment + a villager):
    Militia     Cheap, weak, pulled from the workforce during raids
    Spearman    Anti-large (good vs trolls)
    Archer      Ranged, best on walls/towers
    Swordsman   Solid all-rounder
    Knight      Fast heavy cavalry (City tier)

  Behavior:
    - AUTO: squads hold a rally point, engage enemies within range, return.
    - MANUAL: select squad -> move / attack / hold; returns to auto when
      released.
  Villagers flee to their home or the Keep when raiders are near.
  Wounded troops heal at barracks/hospital; dead troops = lost citizens.


10. ENEMIES (MONSTER RAIDS)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  Raids arrive on a timer with a warning horn + direction indicator.
  Raid strength scales with population, wealth and time elapsed.

  Enemy types:
    Goblin          Fast, weak, steals goods and flees       (early)
    Goblin Archer   Ranged harassment                        (early)
    Orc Warrior     Tough melee, attacks buildings           (mid)
    Orc Shaman      Heals/buffs orcs                         (mid)
    Troll           Very slow, huge HP, smashes walls        (late)
    Wolf Riders     Fast flankers, target outlying farms     (mid)
    Dragon          Boss; flies over walls, burns buildings  (final)

  Enemy goals (varies by type): loot storage, burn buildings, kill villagers,
  reach the Keep. Burning buildings spread fire if not put out (well coverage).


11. CONTROLS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  Pan camera        WASD / Arrow keys / Middle-mouse drag
  Zoom              Mouse wheel (zooms to cursor)
  Build             R Road, Tab cycles categories, 1-9 picks a building
  Select            Left-click a building to inspect it
  Demolish          X (50% refund)
  Cancel            Right-click / Esc
  Pause / speed     Space / top-bar buttons (1x, 2x, 4x)
  Squads            Click a troop / drag a box to select; right-click to
                    move or attack; G = guard here; Ctrl+1-9 = squad N
  Debug             F9 calls a raid now (30s warning)
  Planned: overlays
  (road access, needs coverage, happiness heatmap).


12. UI
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  - Top bar: resources, population/housing, workers/jobs, speed.
  - Bottom bar: build menu (will become categorized tabs:
    Roads / Housing / Resources / Food / Industry / Military / Civic).
  - Hover info: tile or building details.
  - Planned: building inspector panel, raid warning banner, tier progress,
    research screen, overlays, minimap.


13. ART DIRECTION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  - 32x32 top-down pixel art, warm medieval palette, readable when zoomed out.
  - LOCKED STYLE (art test, see docs/style_test.png):
      - Buildings in 3/4 view (front wall + door visible), black outlines,
        basic shading, warm thatch/timber tones.
      - Terrain: muted olive grass, lineless, basic shading; roads autotile
        (Wang corner tiles) over grass.
      - Characters ~16x31 px figures on a 48x48 canvas, black outline.
  - Generated with PixelLab; the game still draws placeholder shapes until
    the M6 art pass. Approved assets live in assets/.
  - Asset list (priority order):
      1. Terrain tileset (grass, forest, water, sand, stone, road)
         [grass + road DONE]
      2. Villager (4-direction walk), carrying variants  [villager DONE]
      3. Keep, House, Woodcutter, Quarry, Stockpile      [house DONE]
      4. Goblin, brute, guard tower, barracks, troops
      5. Farm/mill/bakery chain
  - PixelLab budget: 24 of 40 trial generations left. Real costs: tileset
    ~3-4, standard character 1, 64x64 Pro Flash image 5, 1-direction object
    20-40 (avoid). Chain new tilesets from existing base tile IDs.


14. TECHNICAL ARCHITECTURE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  scripts/autoload/game_state.gd       Stock, capacity, population, speed,
                                       game over
  scripts/data/                        Data tables: building_defs, item_defs,
                                       enemy_defs, unit_defs
  scripts/world/terrain.gd             Terrain types, rules, tileset atlas
  scripts/world/world_map.gd           Grid, generation, roads, fields,
                                       placement, A*, enemy registry
  scripts/world/building.gd            Placed building (health, towers)
  scripts/agents/villager.gd           Citizen agent (work loops, hiding)
  scripts/agents/enemy.gd              Raider agent (thief / wrecker)
  scripts/agents/troop.gd              Soldier agent (auto / manual)
  scripts/combat/                      Health, projectiles
  scripts/systems/citizen_manager.gd   Spawning, jobs, food, tax, drafting
  scripts/systems/raid_director.gd     Raid schedule, spawning, repair
  scripts/systems/military.gd          Training, squads, upkeep
  scripts/systems/squad.gd             Squad modes, orders, formation
  scripts/controllers/                 Camera, build tools, unit control
  scripts/ui/                          HUD, building panel, squad panel,
                                       raid arrow, game over

  Notes:
    - Pathfinding: AStarGrid2D, road weight 1.0, terrain 2.0–3.5.
    - Stock is pooled kingdom-wide; per-building inventories are backlog.
    - Performance target: 300+ agents; move to flow fields / path caching /
      staggered updates if needed.
    - Save/load planned once core systems stabilize.
