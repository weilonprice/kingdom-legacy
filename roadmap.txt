Last updated: 2026-09-22


GUIDING RULES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  - Keep the game playable at the end of every milestone.
  - Systems first, art later: placeholder shapes until a system is locked in.
  - Data-driven: new buildings/units/enemies should mostly be new entries in
    a defs file, not new code.
  - Prove each milestone with tools/verify (real input, screenshots; see
    .claude/skills/verify-kingdom-legacy) plus headless smoke tests for long
    simulations, before playtesting by hand. Add a feature file + scenario
    for new player-facing features.
  - Spend PixelLab generations only on assets whose size/role is final.

M1 — FOOD & GROWTH                                    [DONE]
================================================================================
  Systems
  [x] (M) Generic "produce" work type: worker fetches input from storage,
          works work_time at the building, hauls output to storage
          (building_defs keys: work, input, output, work_time)
  [x] (S) Output space reservation so full storage can't deadlock a chain
  [x] (M) Storage capacity limits per category (materials / food)
          NOTE: stock is pooled kingdom-wide; villagers still walk goods to
          the nearest storage accepting that category. True per-building
          inventories are deferred (see BACKLOG).
  [x] (M) Workers fetch inputs from storage, deliver outputs to storage
          -> villager.gd
  [x] (S) Food resource type(s): wheat, flour, bread, fish
          -> scripts/data/item_defs.gd

  Buildings
  [x] (M) Farm: tills up to 8 field tiles around it, plant -> grow (45s)
          -> harvest 3 wheat (new FIELD terrain with 3 growth visuals)
  [x] (S) Mill: wheat -> flour
  [x] (S) Bakery: flour -> bread
  [x] (S) Fisher's Hut: must be placed next to water -> fish
  [x] (S) Granary: food-only storage
  [x] (S) Well: placeable, shows coverage radius (no effect until M5)

  Population
  [x] (M) Food consumption: each villager eats periodically from storage
  [x] (S) Growth gated by food: no new villagers if food stock is 0
  [x] (S) Starvation: villagers leave the town after X time without food
  [x] (S) HUD: food total + "days of food" estimate

  UI
  [x] (M) Building inspector panel (click a building): workers, inventory,
          status ("waiting for flour", "no road", "storage full")
          -> scripts/ui/building_panel.gd
  [x] (S) Categorized build menu tabs (Roads/Housing/Resources/Food)

  Done when: a town of 20+ runs a farm->mill->bakery chain and grows only
  while bread keeps up; inspector shows why a building is idle.

  [x] (M) House needs + happiness score per household (0-100): needs met,

  [x] (S) Unhappiness: slower work (<40), no move-ins (<35), residents leave

  [x] (M) Overlays (O): happiness heatmap, service coverage, road access

  [x] (S) Tier requirements use needs: Town needs 4 Townhouses; City needs
          6 Manors and 60 happiness; Kingdom needs 65 happiness
  [x] (M) Per-building storage: each storage building holds its own goods

          they reach; destroyed stores lose their goods  -> systems/stock.gd
  [x] (M) Workplace output piles; producers fetch inputs from storage or
          another workplace's pile (bakers take flour straight from the mill)
  [x] (M) Haulers: Carter's Yard (3 jobs) empties piles into storage and
          supplies producers; without haulers workers self-haul full piles
  [x] (S) Day/night cycle (150s day / 50s night): world tint, villagers

  Done when: a city's happiness visibly depends on layout and services,

PHASE 2 — SIMULATION DIRECTION (decided 2026-09-24)
================================================================================
  The game shifts toward simulation: a big castle at the heart of the
  kingdom, better walls, deeper systems, zoom that works on a trackpad.
  Decisions: design.txt section 17.

  M21  Camera: trackpad pinch and two-finger pan, +/- keys, on-screen zoom
       buttons, smooth zoom, zoom out to the whole map   [DONE]
  M22  The castle grows: Keep 5x5 -> Castle 7x7 -> Citadel 9x9, upgrades
       are construction projects (materials hauled in, builders at work),
       new art for each stage; the map start clears room for it   [DONE]
  M23  The royal family and the treasury: a named lord, spouse and heir
       living in the castle, succession when the lord dies; the treasury
       and reserve stores kept in the castle; raiders who break in steal
       and burn them
  M24  Better wall building: gatehouses, automatic corner towers,
       cost/length preview while dragging
  M25  People's lives: ages, families, births, children growing into
       workers, old age and death, named citizens to follow
  M26  Stats & graphs: a kingdom ledger with graphs over time and
       per-building production
  M27  Health & disease: illness in crowded or dirty quarters, healers,
       clean water, plague events

  Not chosen for now: castle damage penalties, castle garrison/towers,
  castle curtain wall, walls as a tier requirement, wall art pass,
  decrees & laws.

  M21 done 2026-09-24 (camera_controller.gd, HUD zoom buttons beside the
  minimap; whole map at ~0.21x runs ~70 fps on a Normal map at 4x).
